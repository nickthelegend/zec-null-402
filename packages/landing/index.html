Frequently asked questions

What a ticket hides, and who you still trust.

Is it trustless?

No. The gateway is trusted for liveness and its state. Anchors make its leaf and nullifier sets auditable after the fact; they do not stop a dishonest gateway in the moment.

What does the gateway learn per call?

That some unspent note in the pool paid, not which. In the run every call came from one depositor's notes, so unlinkability between users needs several depositors.

Can a ticket give change?

No. A note pays one call of its full value. A dearer endpoint needs a note of its own.

Is it live?

On Zcash testnet, with every transaction linked above. Single-party Groth16 setup, not audited. A portfolio piece; the Zecathon entry is Kage (Shielded x402).

Print your own tickets

Circuits, the gateway, an SDK and an MCP server. Proofs in the tests are real Groth16 proofs; the chain is real Zcash testnet.
