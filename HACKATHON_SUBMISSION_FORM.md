## Review fixes
- Oct 21: a strict review (`docs/JUDGE-2026-10-21.md`) found double spends in the Oct 20 gateway: a spent note paid again with a re-encoded nullifier, and parallel requests with one payment were all paid. Fixed, each with a test that fails on the old code, together with a relay attack on the client, an unauthenticated wallet-spending anchor route, the viewing key on `/api/info` and unbounded memory.
- Oct 22, pass 2: clients prove only against a root they read themselves in a recent on-chain anchor (lightwalletd + the published anchor viewing key); refused before proving otherwise. Gateway state moved to SQLite (restart keeps challenges and spent nullifiers; two processes on one database pay a note once).
- Oct 22, ultra round (two more judges on `773ffc5`): signed anchors and a client that scans the anchor address over its window, so a gateway cannot hand a client its own root without putting a second signed root on-chain, which clients refuse (`ANCHOR_COMPETING`), and that proves against the newest anchor whichever is named; periodic re-anchoring with retries; one anchor send at a time; the SDK fetches only its gateway; a locked notes file; fail-closed settings; bounded per-request work; a total deposit budget; copy corrected (the agent's order came from the operator's prompt; the Oct 20 double credit of `0b12bfc6` is disclosed). 38 new tests; 37 fail on `773ffc5`.

## Honest scope
Off-chain verifier (Zcash has no contracts): the gateway is trusted for liveness and its nullifier set and must keep anchoring (10,000 zat + fee per anchor). Clients trust their lightwalletd for anchors and see a competing anchor only once it serves that block; the gateway can still time its anchors and sees network addresses. New notes wait for the next anchor. Anchors carry the spent count under a public viewing key, so they reveal how many calls were paid between anchors (single calls at low traffic). Both runs had a single depositor. Single-party setup. Testnet only. Not audited. Built on null-402 / near-null-402 (same author).
