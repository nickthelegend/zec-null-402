How it works

Zcash has no contracts, so the gateway is the pool.

The deposit stays on-chain; per-call checks happen off-chain in zero knowledge.

  1. 01

    Deposit once

    One shielded transaction credits your notes under the gateway's viewing key.

  2. 02

    Prove per call

    On 402, the client reads on-chain anchors through its own lightwalletd and proves an unspent note.

  3. 03

    Anchor

    The gateway posts its root in a shielded memo so clients that see the same anchor prove against the same root.
